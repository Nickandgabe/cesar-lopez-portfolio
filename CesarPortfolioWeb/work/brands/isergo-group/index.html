01
01Overview

Corporate weight, engineered clarity.

ISERGO Group's hexagon-and-droplet mark reads like a technical diagram — because it's meant to. The identity is built for a multi-division industrial group that needs to feel unified across engineering, resources, and operations.

Deep navy carries the authority of a group-level parent brand, while a single light-blue accent keeps the system from feeling heavy — reserved for the word "group" and key highlights only.

ToneCorporate, precise, unified
VoiceFormal, structured, confident
Best forIndustrial groups, holding companies, B2B
AvoidMultiple accent colors, informal type

What not to do.

✕
