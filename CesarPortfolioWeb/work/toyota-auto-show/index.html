About the project

Why we built it

The Toyota Virtual Auto Show was created in response to the COVID-19 pandemic, which made it difficult or impossible for people to attend in-person auto shows. The problem it aimed to solve: how to give people the opportunity to experience Toyota's latest products and technology without physically stepping onto a showroom floor.

The virtual auto show provides an online experience that comes as close as possible to attending in person — complete with interactive video, virtual tours, and the ability to view and learn about Toyota's latest vehicles and technology.

The problem

What Toyota was up against

Some of the specific challenges Toyota faced in creating the virtual auto show:

01

Designing an online platform that's easy to use and navigate for users of every level of technical proficiency.

02

Creating high-quality 3D models and interactive exhibits that accurately represent Toyota's latest products and technology.

03

Developing a content strategy that delivers the information and engagement people expect from an auto show, with real opportunities for interaction and participation.

04

Ensuring the virtual auto show is accessible to a wide range of users, including those with disabilities or limited internet access.

The solution

Pure driving pleasure,
from anywhere

A digital alternative to traditional auto shows — which can be costly, time-consuming, and limited by physical space. Through the Toyota Virtual Auto Show, users access immersive 3D models of Toyota's vehicles, informative videos, interactive demos, and live streaming events showcasing the latest innovations.

By leveraging digital technology, the platform gives Toyota a cost-effective, scalable way to reach a wider audience, engage customers in new ways, and collect valuable data on behavior and preferences to inform future product and marketing strategy.
